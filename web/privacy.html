File Manager

Android · package com.homilabs.filemanager · Section updated 9 November 2026 (version 1.0.0)

File Manager itself collects no personal data. It reads the files and folders on your phone so it can show them and copy, move, rename, delete or share them for you. Your files and their names never leave your phone through File Manager: nothing is uploaded to HomiLabs or anyone else. A file leaves your phone only when you tap Share and pick another app to send it with. There is no account, no login and no analytics. File Manager is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Permissions

  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 11 and newer): the core of a file manager; you switch it on in Android's settings, and it is used only to show and manage your files on your phone.
  • Storage (READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 10 and older only): the same purpose on older phones.
  • The ad SDK adds the standard permissions it needs: internet and network state (to fetch ads) and the advertising ID.

File Manager does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone access.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, File Manager asks for your consent before any personalised ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time under About → Ad privacy options.
  • You can switch All files access off at any time in your phone's settings; File Manager then cannot show your files.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (see section 3 above).

Google's use of this data is covered by its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
